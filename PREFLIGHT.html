Press RUN CHECKS. The drive and relay checks need one click each because the browser will only open a folder picker or hit the network from a real button press.
DRIVE FINGERPRINT   —
Unlocked once the checks come back clear. OUTPOST holds no vault, so it opens once the checks have run.
WHAT THIS CANNOT SEE
  • It cannot see a keylogger, a screen recorder, or anything reading your keystrokes. A web page has no way to look at the machine it is running on.
  • It cannot tell whether this PC copied files off the drive. Reads leave no trace on the drive.
  • A fingerprint mismatch proves something wrote to the drive. A fingerprint match proves nothing wrote to it in a way that also failed to update the manifest — someone who knows FieldKit is on this stick could do both.
  • If the machine belongs to someone else and you would not type your bank password into it, do not type your vault passphrase into it either. No test on this page changes that.
WHAT IT IS ACTUALLY FOR
  • Catching a PC that wrote to your drive — an autorun dropper, a modified core file, a half-finished copy.
  • Catching a browser that has something sitting between FieldKit and the crypto it depends on.
  • Catching FieldKit data left behind on this PC by an earlier session, and wiping it.
  • Confirming the relay is the relay, and that this machine's clock is close enough for TLS to work.
  • Saying which of your relay addresses this network lets through, and whether the streamed browser and RUNNER will work here (WebSockets, video and sound decoding).
